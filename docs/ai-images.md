# Labelling AI-Generated and AI-Modified Images

## Purpose

Users must be clearly informed when a property image has been generated or modified with artificial intelligence. The label improves transparency and must remain visible across all views and screen sizes.

## Required AI Badge

Every image generated or modified with AI must display an `AI` badge in the **bottom-right corner** of the image.

This applies to every way an image is presented on the site, including:

- property card images
- property page image galleries
- enlarged images and lightbox views
- mobile and desktop views

The badge must be displayed as an overlay on the image, not as text outside it. Its position must be anchored to the image container so that it remains in the bottom-right corner when the image is cropped or the screen size changes.

## When to Display the Label

Always display the badge when an image is:

- generated entirely with AI or
- modified with AI in a way that changes its content, such as adding, removing, or changing a room, furniture, materials, or other property features.

Technical processing alone, such as image compression, resizing, or standard cropping, does not require an AI badge.

## Checklist

Before publishing, make sure that:

- every AI image has an AI badge;
- the badge is visible in the bottom-right corner in all views;
- the badge remains visible across different image aspect ratios and screen sizes; and
- regular images that have not been processed with AI do not receive the label.
